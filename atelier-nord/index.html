How we work

From first sketch to handover

  1. Stage 1 · 3–4 weeks

    Listen

    Site visits at different times of day, and long conversations about how you actually live.

  2. Stage 2 · 6–8 weeks

    Sketch

    Hand drawings and card models first. You see three directions before we touch a computer.

  3. Stage 3 · 3–6 months

    Detail

    Every joint, window reveal and handrail drawn at 1:5, priced with the builder before we build.

  4. Stage 4 · on site

    Build

    We visit site every week until handover, and come back one year later to see how it aged.
