A small studio with long patience.

Fourteen people, one model-making workshop and a shared lunch table, in a former print works on Nørrebro.

We believe a good building is mostly invisible: you notice the morning light, the warm floor, the quiet. Not the architect.

Atelier Nord was founded in 2011 by Ida Holm and Rasmus Lund after a decade at larger Copenhagen practices. They wanted a studio small enough that the people drawing the details also visit the site.

Today we work on homes, affordable housing, hotels and small cultural buildings. Most of our projects come from people who visited a building we designed and asked who made it.

We build with timber wherever the structure allows, reuse what is already on site, and design for repair. Every project is measured against a carbon budget agreed with the client at the start.
