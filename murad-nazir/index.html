Profile

He works where foreign technology, Pakistani institutions and private capital meet: campus energy systems, electric vehicles, skills, power and large-scale development.

As Chief Executive Officer of Plotify (Private) Limited, he heads the company, sets its strategy, takes its final commercial decisions and signs on its behalf. Under his leadership the company has entered formal engagements with a federal public sector university, a Chinese industrial group and the Special Investment Facilitation Council.

His work brings Chinese industrial partners such as Shandong Xinxu Group together with Quaid-i-Azam University, the International Islamic University Islamabad, Fauji Foundation and the Ministry of Industries & Production.

Initiatives

Work led through Plotify, from campus energy systems, power and electric vehicles to skills, food-processing trade and large-scale development.

Smart, shared electric mobility on campus

On 24 July 2026 he signed a five-year tripartite MOU with Quaid-i-Azam University and Shandong Xinxu International Corporation Pakistan Limited. A second agreement, with E-GO Electric Bike (Private) Limited, launched a Smart Shared Electric Bike Pilot Scheme for students, faculty and staff, initiated and facilitated by Plotify.

The system brings e-bikes, smart docking stations and EV charging to departments and hostels, and is extending to the International Islamic University Islamabad through the Green E-Bike Initiative.

  • Shared e-bikes
  • Electric campus buses
  • Electric rickshaws
  • Solarisation of buildings

Plotify is the central point of coordination, from energy audits and feasibility studies to phased pilot deployment.

A strategic partnership for projects across Pakistan

Plotify (Private) Limited and Shandong Xinxu International Corporation have formalised a consultancy agreement for nationwide project facilitation, business development and strategic consultancy.

  • Project facilitation
  • Business development
  • Market access
  • Stakeholder engagement

The agreement sets Plotify as the partner for creating new business opportunities in Pakistan and building long-term projects with the group.

Opening the power and energy sector to new partners

He joined the senior leadership of Fauji Foundation’s Power Vertical and Shandong Xinxu Group to explore collaboration and future project opportunities between the two organisations.

  • Power
  • Energy
  • Industry
  • Technology
  • Industrial zones

Plotify and Shandong Xinxu Group followed with a site visit to the Foundation Renewable & Technology Zone (FRTZ) to assess its infrastructure and development plans, and to identify areas for investment, technology integration and industrial cooperation.

Discussions covered potential joint projects and long-term collaboration between Fauji Foundation, Power AWT and Shandong Xinxu Group, connecting Chinese technology and industrial capability with opportunities in Pakistan.

Industry-aligned skills at a federal university

He brought to Pakistan a proposal by Shandong Xinxu Group Corporation to establish and upgrade competency-based, technology-driven training facilities at Quaid-i-Azam University. Letters of Intent followed to the Vice Chancellor on 19 August 2026 and to the Special Investment Facilitation Council on 21 August 2026.

  • Industrial automation
  • Smart manufacturing
  • Industry 4.0 systems
  • Applied engineering

Plotify is named in both letters as consultant, facilitator and coordinating entity. The initiative is aligned with the National TVET Policy 2018 and the National Education Policy 2024.

Electric vehicles, localised in Pakistan

Building Pakistan’s electric vehicle ecosystem

Through Plotify he is leading the introduction and phased localisation of electric two-wheelers in Pakistan with Chinese partners.

With representatives of Shandong Xinxu Group and Cherry Group, he met the Joint Secretary of the Ministry of Industries & Production on EV cooperation and localisation, including the upcoming launch of a new four-wheeler EV by Cherry Group in collaboration with Shandong Xinxu Group.

  • Technology cooperation
  • Investment and localisation
  • Lower fuel import bill
  • Lower urban emissions
  • Local assembly jobs

Displacing imported fuel in the two-wheeler segment has a measurable effect on both energy security and the balance of payments.

Pakistan–China livestock and food-processing value chain

Trade linkages in the livestock sector

Plotify led a visit with Shandong Xinxu Group to Fauji Meat Limited to explore cooperation in the offal sector, including an overview of the facility, its operations and existing processing capabilities.

  • Collaboration
  • Sourcing
  • International markets

The engagement is part of a wider effort to build Pakistan–China industrial and trade links across the livestock and food-processing value chain.
