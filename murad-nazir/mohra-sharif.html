Role

He exercises delegated executive authority over the affairs of the Darbar on behalf of the Sajjada Nashin, and is the principal line between the devotee network and the Sajjada Nashin.

  • Directs administration, events, public engagement and community relations for the Darbar.
  • Leads the countrywide mobilisation for the annual Urs, congregations, rallies and other major events, including logistics, crowd management, volunteers and liaison with civil administration.
  • Community representatives, district officials and delegations of followers deal with him as decision maker on the Darbar’s day-to-day affairs.
  • Carries the moderate Sufi tradition of Mohra Sharif and discourages sectarian friction within the community.

Gatherings and cohesion

Devotional gatherings, turned into occasions for cohesion, welfare and peaceful collective conduct.

The annual Urs and major events

Gatherings on this scale require planning across administration, logistics, crowd management, security liaison and communication with district authorities. He directs that work, and with it the transmission of a moderate Sufi tradition to a large and dispersed audience.
