Plumbing and HVAC problems we see most in Mesa

Mesa's water averages around 17 grains per gallon of hardness, one of the highest levels in the Valley. That mineral build-up is behind most of the calls we get here: water heaters failing early, scaled-up fixtures, and pinhole leaks in the copper pipes found in many 1970s and 80s homes around Dobson Ranch and Alta Mesa.

In summer, it's air conditioning. When it's 110°F, an AC that stops cooling is an emergency, especially for older neighbours and pets. We keep the parts that fail most often in Mesa heat (capacitors, contactors and fan motors) on every truck.
