Signs your water heater needs help

Rusty or smelly waterA corroding anode rod or tank, common with Mesa's hard water.
Popping or rumbling noisesMineral sediment built up on the bottom of the tank.
Water around the baseA leaking valve, fitting, or a tank near the end of its life.

Repair or replace?

Tank heaters in the East Valley usually last 8 to 10 years, less without regular flushing. If yours is under 8 years old, a repair is almost always the better value. Your technician will show you both options and prices before any work begins.

Water heater FAQ

How long does a water heater last in Arizona?

Usually 8 to 10 years for a tank heater. Hard water shortens that, so we recommend flushing the tank once a year.

Can you replace my water heater today?

Yes. We stock 40 and 50 gallon gas and electric tanks on our trucks and in our Mesa warehouse, and most replacements are finished the same day.

Is tankless worth it?

For larger households, often yes: endless hot water, about 20 years of life, and lower energy bills. We'll size it for your home and show you the payback.
